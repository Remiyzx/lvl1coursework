📌 Some Important Notes & Script Rules

A
Make sure the content matches the topic (Péngyou, nǐ hǎo!).
B
The number of scenes must match the number of group members (e.g., a group of 3 must have at least 3 scenes).
C
Not every member has to appear in every scene, but each person must speak at least 15 valid sentences.
D
Only a complete sentence (S + V + O or S + adv + A) with 3 or more Pinyin syllables counts as one line.
E
Please highlight any extra vocabulary used (maximum 3 extra words allowed; proper nouns are exempt).
F
Write or type everything in the template and submit a hard copy at the end.
  • Video Length & Subtitles: Must be 3–4 minutes total (including opening & closing credits ≤ 1 min), with English or Malay subtitles only (no Pinyin subtitles).
  • Syllabus Scope: Use KuaiLeXueHanYu Lessons 1–4 and Textbook Lessons 1–4 only (maximum 3 highlighted extra words allowed).

🎬 Suggested Storyline Outline

🚀 Creative Story Flow: Friends greet each other on campus, introduce a new classmate, chat about their busy families and favorite hobbies, and invite everyone out for a fun hangout!
SCENE 01

Greeting & Basic Self-Introduction

  • Greeting / Saying hello e.g. nǐ hǎo, nǐ hǎo ma ...
  • Self-introduction (name) e.g. jiào, shénme, zhè, nà, shéi ...
  • Nationality / Where you are from e.g. nǎ, guó, rén, ma, ne ...
  • Simple identity info e.g. shì, Mǎláixīyà Shābā Dàxué, xuéyuàn, xuésheng, xuéxí, Hànyǔ ...
SCENE 02

Asking About Family & Work

  • Family members e.g. wàipó, nǎinai, bàba, māma, gēge, jiějie, dìdi, mèimei, máng ...
  • Asking about job / occupation e.g. xuésheng, lǎoshī, yīshēng, jìzhě ...
SCENE 03

Interests & Likes

  • Asking about hobbies e.g. xǐhuan, kàn diànyǐng, chànggē ...
  • Saying likes / dislikes e.g. xǐhuan, bù xǐhuan, hē kāfēi/kělè, chī hànbǎo/píngguǒ ...
SCENE 04

Inviting Someone to Go Somewhere or Do Something

  • Inviting to go somewhere e.g. yào, qù, shāngchǎng, cāntīng, sùshè, dàxué ...
  • Inviting to do an activity e.g. qù, kàn diànyǐng, chànggē, hē kāfēi, chī hànbǎo ...
  • Making simple friendly comments & Ending politely e.g. rènshi, gāoxìng ...
