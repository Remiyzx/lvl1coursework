📌 Some Important Notes & Script Rules

A
Make sure the content matches the topic (Péngyou, nǐ hǎo!).
B
The number of scenes must match the number of group members (e.g., a group of 3 = at least 3 scenes).
C
Not every member has to appear in every scene, but each person must speak at least 15 valid sentences.
D
Only a complete sentence (S + V + O or S + adv + A) with 3 or more Pinyin syllables counts as one line.
E
Please highlight any extra vocabulary used (maximum 3 extra words; proper nouns exempt).
F
Write or type everything in the official template and submit a hard copy at the end.
  • Video Length & Subtitles: Must be 3–4 minutes total (credits ≤ 1 min), with English or Malay subtitles only (no Pinyin subtitles).
  • Syllabus Scope: Use KuaiLeXueHanYu Lessons 1–4 and Textbook Lessons 1–4 only (maximum 3 highlighted extra words allowed).

🎬 Suggested Storyline Outline

🚀 Creative Story Flow: Friends greet each other on campus, introduce a new classmate, chat about their busy families and favorite hobbies, and invite everyone out for a fun hangout!
SCENE 01

Greeting & Basic Self-Introduction

  • Friends meet on campus and say hello using greetings like nǐ hǎo and nǐ hǎo ma, then spark the conversation by asking "Who is he/she/this/that?" (tā / zhè / nà shì shéi) and "Don't you know him/her?" (nǐ bù rènshi ma) Lessons 1 & 3
  • Introduce yourselves and ask each other's names using jiào shénme Lesson 3
  • Ask about each other's nationality and where everyone is from (nǎ guó rén, ma, ne) and share simple identity details—such as being a student (shì xuésheng) at Mǎláixīyà Shābā Dàxué, your faculty (xuéyuàn), and studying Mandarin (xuéxí Hànyǔ) or other languages learned in the textbook (e.g., Yīngyǔ, Fǎyǔ) Lessons 3 & 4
SCENE 02

Asking About Family & Work

  • Chat about your family members—such as wàipó, nǎinai, bàba, māma, gēge, jiějie, dìdi, and mèimei—and ask whether they are busy (máng) Lesson 2
    ⚠️ Note on Sibling Quantity: Do not state the number of siblings yet, as measure words are only taught in Level II. If you really want to use quantities, follow the structure Nu + MW + N (e.g., liǎng gè jiějie 两个姐姐, yī gè dìdi 一个弟弟) and highlight gè as an extra word!
  • Ask about each other's jobs and occupations, discussing whether family members or friends are a student (xuésheng), lecturer (lǎoshī), doctor (yīshēng), or journalist (jìzhě) Lesson 4
SCENE 03

Interests & Likes

  • Ask about each other's hobbies and favorite activities, such as watching movies (kàn diànyǐng) or singing (chànggē) Lesson 4
  • Share what everyone likes and dislikes (xǐhuan / bù xǐhuan), including drinking coffee or cola (hē kāfēi / kělè) and eating hamburgers or apples (chī hànbǎo / píngguǒ) Lesson 4
SCENE 04

Inviting Someone to Go Somewhere or Do Something

  • Invite your friends to go somewhere together (yào qù), such as the mall (shāngchǎng), cafeteria (cāntīng), hostel (sùshè), or university (dàxué) Lesson 4
  • Suggest fun activities to do together, like going to watch a movie (qù kàn diànyǐng), singing (chànggē), drinking coffee (hē kāfēi), or eating hamburgers (chī hànbǎo) Lesson 4
  • Wrap up the conversation with friendly comments and polite farewells, saying how happy you are to meet each other (rènshi, gāoxìng) Lesson 4
